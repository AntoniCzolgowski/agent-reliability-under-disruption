## Questions

1. What does a city look like during an emergency compared with a normal week: where do people stop going, and where do they go instead?
2. Are people who are seen less often during a disruption really moving less, or are they simply less visible to whoever is recording them?
3. Do people fall into recognisable kinds of movers, such as regular commuters and people who roam widely, and do some kinds keep their patterns in a disruption while others scatter?
4. How few traits are enough to tell one person's routine from another's?
5. Can the kind of place someone is at, such as a station, a shop, a school or a park, be guessed from where and when they are seen, without anyone saying so?
6. From what a person has just done, can one tell whether they are about to move on or stay put, and how far the next move will take them?
7. Does knowing what kinds of places surround a person help describe their ordinary days, and does that knowledge still help once routines break?
8. Researchers now build simulated cities out of agents, each one modelled on a real person. How faithfully can such an agent, built only from that person's everyday routine, describe them on an ordinary day?
9. When an emergency interrupts daily life, how quickly does the agent stop being a trustworthy description of its person, and did its record in normal times give any warning?
10. How much fresh evidence from the days after the disruption does the agent need before it is reliable again, and does it matter how it takes that evidence in or only how much of it there is?
11. In a simulation an agent has to keep going by itself, with nobody correcting it along the way. How long can it run before it drifts from what its person really did, and does it drift sooner during an emergency?
12. An agent goes out of date as its person's life moves on, but updating it is computationally expensive, so it can only be done a few times. Can it learn, from ordinary weeks alone, to notice when it is getting its person wrong and update itself in time? And does that still work the first time an emergency arrives?
